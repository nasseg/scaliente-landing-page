# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Independent Shopify merchants and small e-commerce teams who need to understand actual profitability without rebuilding it manually in spreadsheets. They arrive with limited attention, often between operational decisions, and need to judge quickly whether Scaliente is credible, relevant to their store, and easy to adopt.

## Product Purpose

Scaliente is a public marketing site for a Shopify profit-tracking product. The site must make the product's operational value legible, demonstrate how revenue and costs become a trustworthy profit view, and move qualified visitors toward starting a trial or exploring a plan. Success means informed conversion without overstating capabilities, pricing, results, or social proof.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, energetic. The experience should feel like a well-made operating instrument: confident enough to be memorable, calm enough to inspect, and transparent enough to earn trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic 2024–2026 SaaS landing-page grammar: purple or orange gradient text, decorative glassmorphism everywhere, floating-icon card grids, aurora blobs, identical reveal animations, oversized hero metrics, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, and anonymous “AI-made” polish. Preserve the verified customer reviews, rating, and their SEO structured data. Avoid fake dashboards, inflated statistics, superlatives, and claims the product source cannot verify.

## Design Principles

1. Show the operating truth: product evidence and clear information hierarchy should carry the story.
2. Make orange a signal, not decoration: reserve it for actions, focus, and decisive emphasis.
3. Build rhythm, not repetition: each section should advance the narrative with its own appropriate composition.
4. Earn trust through restraint: remove decorative effects that compete with comprehension or performance.
5. Design for the hurried merchant: the proposition, proof, pricing path, and next action must remain obvious on every viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, 44px touch targets, sufficient contrast, readable line lengths, reduced-motion alternatives, and resilient layouts for French, English, and German copy. The core experience must remain complete without animation or WebGL effects.
